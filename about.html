About

I'm an M.Sc. Economics (Data Analytics) student at Symbiosis School of Economics, Pune, with a foundation in economic theory built during my B.Sc. Economics Honours at The Bhawanipur Education Society College, University of Calcutta. My work sits at the intersection of econometrics and applied data analysis — using Python, R, SQL and Stata to move from raw datasets to defensible conclusions.

My academic projects so far have looked at how inflation and unemployment moved through the 2008 financial crisis, and at what drives democratic destabilisation across countries. I'm eager to bring that same analytical rigour to business analysis and research roles, while continuing to learn.
